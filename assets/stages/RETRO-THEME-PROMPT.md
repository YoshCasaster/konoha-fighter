# Retro stage theme

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The source image is stored at `source/retro-temple.png`; `npm run ui:build` exports the 1600×900 WebP backgrounds and UI sprite strips used by the game.

Prompt: A wide retro 1v1 fighting-game background showing an Indonesian-inspired martial arts arena at night. Symmetrical stone courtyard, monumental split gate and temple silhouette, blue moon and storm clouds, red and blue braziers, distant city lights, banners without writing, wet reflective floor, atmospheric mist. Polished 16-bit arcade pixel art with navy, crimson, and gold colors. No people, fighters, interface, text, logos, or watermark; active outer edges and readable central negative space.
